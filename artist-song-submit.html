What happens next?

Your song will initially be marked as pending. Local Shuffle will review the submission before activating it.

An active song may be randomly selected when a customer redeems a Local Shuffle code.

Only one song from an artist can be active in the Local Shuffle rotation at a time.

Artist identity and account verification will be connected before this submission system is opened to the public.
