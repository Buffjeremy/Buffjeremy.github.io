Local Shuffle Business Application

Bring local music to your customers with Local Shuffle.

Customers purchase a qualifying item at your business and can add $1 to receive a randomly selected song from a local artist. Your business provides the redemption code and the customer enters that code on the Local Shuffle website to receive the MP3.

The Split

  • 75% — Your business
  • 15% — Local artists
  • 10% — Local Shuffle

Submit the application below. Your application will be reviewed before your business is activated.
