How Local Shuffle Works

Local Shuffle is designed to give customers an easy way to discover local music while giving participating businesses an opportunity to support their local music community.

  • The customer pays an additional $1 for a Local Shuffle song.
  • The customer receives a unique redemption code.
  • The code is entered at LocalShuffle.com.
  • Local Shuffle randomly selects a participating local song.
  • The customer receives the song as a downloadable MP3.

The current proposed distribution of each $1 Local Shuffle purchase is:

  • 75% to the participating business
  • 15% distributed equally among participating local artists
  • 10% retained by Local Shuffle

Local Shuffle is currently in its development and testing phase. Program details, payment procedures, and participation requirements may be updated before the program officially launches.
