Become a Local Shuffle Business

Local Shuffle helps local businesses introduce their customers to local music.

Customers purchase a drink, meal, or other qualifying item from your business and can add $1 for a randomly selected song from a local artist.

You provide the customer with a Local Shuffle redemption code. They enter the code on the Local Shuffle website and receive their randomly selected song as an MP3 download.

How the $1 is Split

75% Business
15% Local Artists
10% Local Shuffle

The business keeps 75% of every Local Shuffle dollar. The artist portion is pooled and divided equally among participating artists according to the Local Shuffle artist agreement.
