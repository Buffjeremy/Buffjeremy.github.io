Local Shuffle Business Application

Bring local music to your customers.

How It Works

Customers purchase a qualifying item at your business and can add $1 to receive a randomly selected song from a local artist. Your business provides the redemption code, and the customer enters that code on the Local Shuffle website to receive an MP3 download.

The Revenue Split

  • 75% — Your business
  • 15% — The Local Artist Pool
  • 10% — Local Shuffle operations and development
