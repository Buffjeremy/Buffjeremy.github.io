Great Music Is Happening Right Here

Every day, independent musicians write songs, record albums, and play shows for people who may never have heard their names. Their music deserves opportunities to find an audience beyond the usual streaming recommendations and social media feeds.

At the same time, local coffee shops, breweries, bars, and other independent businesses help create the places where communities come together. They give people a place to meet, relax, and discover new things.

Local Shuffle brings these worlds together. Participating businesses become places where customers can discover a song from a local artist, download it, and explore more of the music they enjoy.

How Local Shuffle Works

01

Visit a Local Business

Stop by a participating coffee shop, brewery, bar, or other local business. Look for the Local Shuffle QR code.

02

Discover a Song

Scan the QR code and get a chance to discover a song from an independent artist. You might find your next favorite band, or simply hear something you haven't heard before.

03

Keep the Music

Download the song file to your device and listen again later. Your discovery doesn't have to end when you leave the business.

04

Support the Artist

Enjoyed what you heard? Find the artist online, explore their releases, follow them on social media, buy their music or merchandise, or catch them at a live show.

Participating businesses can offer the download for free or invite customers to contribute an optional dollar. The goal is to make music discovery easy while creating another way to support the local music community.

More Than Another Streaming Playlist

We live in a world of streaming playlists, endless recommendations, and songs that come and go in seconds. Local Shuffle offers something a little different: an actual song file you can keep on your device.

A download gives you a chance to listen beyond the bar, the coffee shop, or the moment you discovered it. Put it on later, share the artist's name with a friend, or follow the music to its next stop.

And if you love what you hear, there is plenty more to discover. Find the artist online, listen to the rest of their catalog, buy their album or merchandise, follow their social accounts, or go see them perform live.

If the song isn't for you, that's okay too. Discovery means taking a chance on something new. The point is to make that first step easier for everyone.

Built to Support the Local Music Community

Local Shuffle is designed to share the value created by each participating download among the people who make the experience possible.

75%

Participating Business

The largest share goes to the local business hosting the discovery experience.

15%

Participating Artists

A shared pool is distributed equally among eligible artists included in the rotation for the applicable period.

10%

Local Shuffle

Supports the platform and the work needed to keep the project running and growing.

These percentages describe the planned allocation of each dollar contributed through the program. They do not represent a guarantee of earnings for any individual business or artist.

Why Local Businesses Matter

Local businesses are more than places to buy coffee, grab a drink, or meet friends. They help shape the character of a community. Local Shuffle gives participating businesses another way to offer customers something memorable while helping independent musicians reach new listeners.

For customers, it's a simple way to discover music while supporting places they already enjoy. For artists, it creates another potential connection with people who might never have found their music otherwise.

We're starting in the Tampa Bay area, building relationships with local artists and businesses, and working toward a model that can grow into other communities.

Starting Local. Growing Together.

Local Shuffle is being built around a simple idea: music discovery can be more personal, more local, and more connected to the places where people spend their time.

We're starting small, learning what works, and building a community of artists, businesses, and music fans who believe that great music is worth discovering.

Every participating business, every artist who contributes a song, and every customer who gives something new a listen helps move that idea forward.
