Local Shuffle turns an everyday stop at your favorite local business into a chance to discover new music, download an actual song, and help support the artists who make your community unique.

Great Music Deserves to Be Discovered.

A cup of coffee takes minutes to make. A craft beer might be finished in an hour. But the music playing in the background of your life can take months or even years to create.

Local musicians put countless hours into learning their instruments, writing songs, rehearsing with bandmates, recording, mixing, mastering, and releasing music. Many pay for those efforts out of their own pockets, all for the chance that somebody, somewhere, will hear a song and connect with it.

Yet discovering an unfamiliar local band isn't always easy. You might hesitate to spend money on an album from an artist you've never heard. You might not know which local bands play your kind of music, or where to find them in the first place.

Local Shuffle makes that first listen easier. Instead of asking you to commit to an album or a ticket before you know the music, it gives you a simple way to discover a randomly selected song from a participating local artist.

Maybe it's a band you've never heard of. Maybe it's a song that becomes the soundtrack to your next road trip. Maybe it isn't your thing at all. Either way, you've discovered something new, and the next song could be the one that sticks with you.

How Local Shuffle Works

Local Shuffle connects local musicians, independent businesses, and the people who live, work, and spend time in their community. It is designed to fit naturally into purchases people already make.

1

Visit a Participating Business

Stop by a participating coffee shop, brewery, bar, restaurant, or another local business displaying Local Shuffle information and its QR code or access instructions.

2

Choose to Discover

Scan the code and follow the instructions to participate. Where the business offers the optional $1 Local Shuffle purchase, you can choose to add it to your visit.

3

Get a Real Song

Local Shuffle selects a song from its eligible local music rotation. Download it, give it a listen, and discover an artist you might never have found otherwise.

The participating artist is selected through the Local Shuffle rotation rather than chosen by the customer. That element of surprise is part of the experience.

Why Download a Song?

We live in a world of streaming playlists, endless recommendations, and songs that come and go in seconds. Local Shuffle offers something a little different: an actual song file you can keep on your device.

A download gives you a chance to listen beyond the bar, the coffee shop, or the moment you discovered it. Put it on later, share the artist's name with a friend, or follow the music to its next stop.

And if you love what you hear, there is plenty more to discover. Find the artist online, listen to the rest of their catalog, buy their album or merchandise, follow their social accounts, or go see them perform live.

If the song isn't for you, that's okay too. Discovery means taking a chance on something new. The point is to make that first step easier for everyone.

Supporting the Local Music Community

Local Shuffle is built around the idea that supporting local music doesn't have to mean buying an entire album or attending a show every time. Small contributions from everyday purchases can help create another way for artists to be heard.

Under the planned $1 purchase model, the revenue is allocated among the participating business, the artist community, and the operation of Local Shuffle.

75%

Participating Business

The business keeps the largest share, giving it a direct financial reason to introduce customers to Local Shuffle.

15%

The Artist Pool

A shared pool intended to be divided equally among eligible, active participating artists for the applicable payout period.

10%

Local Shuffle

The remaining share supports operating, maintaining, and developing the service.

These percentages describe the Local Shuffle revenue-allocation model. Actual participation, payment, and payout timing are subject to the program's terms and the activation of payment processing.

Why Local Businesses Matter

Independent businesses are more than places to buy coffee, grab a drink, or meet friends. They are places where communities form, people meet, and local culture comes to life.

Local Shuffle gives participating businesses another way to offer customers something memorable while helping introduce local artists to new listeners. Customers get an opportunity to discover music, businesses keep their share of the purchase, and artists get another chance to reach people.

The goal isn't to replace live music, record stores, streaming services, or buying directly from musicians. It's to create another connection between the local places people visit and the local music they might grow to love.

Starting Local. Growing Together.

Local Shuffle is starting with a Tampa Bay geographic zone, with the goal of building a network of participating artists and businesses before expanding further.

Artists apply to participate, provide information about their released music, and undergo an eligibility and rights review. Participating businesses provide local places where customers can encounter the program. Together, they help make music discovery part of everyday life.

The long-term vision is simple: make it easier for people to discover local music, give independent artists another route to new listeners, and help local businesses play an active role in supporting their community's creative life.
