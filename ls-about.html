Local Shuffle connects independent musicians with the local businesses and music fans who help keep a community's music scene alive.

Great Music Is Happening Right Here

Every community has musicians making great music just around the corner. They are writing songs, recording albums, playing shows, and building something special, often without the attention or resources that bigger acts receive.

Finding those artists can take work. Streaming services are convenient, but their recommendations often lead us back to music we already know. Local Shuffle is designed to make discovering an independent artist a little more personal, unexpected, and fun.

We bring local music into places people already visit, such as coffee shops, breweries, bars, and other participating businesses. A regular stop can become an opportunity to hear something new.

How Local Shuffle Works

1. Visit a Local Business

Look for Local Shuffle at a participating business. Scan its QR code to discover a song from an independent artist in the local music community.

2. Discover a Song

Each discovery gives you a chance to hear an artist you might not have found on your own. The selection is meant to introduce you to something outside your usual rotation.

3. Keep the Music

The idea is simple: receive a downloadable music file that you can keep on your device and listen to later, beyond the place where you discovered it.

4. Support the Artist

If you enjoy the song, find the artist online, explore their other releases, follow them, buy their music or merchandise, and check out a live show.

The planned model allows customers to choose whether to contribute $1 for a song download. The goal is to make music discovery accessible while creating a small source of support for the local businesses and artists involved.

More Than Another Streaming Playlist

We live in a world of streaming playlists, endless recommendations, and songs that come and go in seconds. Local Shuffle offers something a little different: an actual song file you can keep on your device.

A download gives you a chance to listen beyond the bar, the coffee shop, or the moment you discovered it. Put it on later, share the artist's name with a friend, or follow the music to its next stop.

And if you love what you hear, there is plenty more to discover. Find the artist online, listen to the rest of their catalog, buy their album or merchandise, follow their social accounts, or go see them perform live.

If the song isn't for you, that's okay too. Discovery means taking a chance on something new. The point is to make that first step easier for everyone.

Built to Support the Local Music Community

Local Shuffle is intended to create value for everyone involved. Artists get another way to reach listeners. Businesses get a unique experience to offer their customers. Music fans get a chance to discover something new.

Under the planned $1 purchase model, the revenue would be divided like this:

  • 75% — Participating Business
    The business hosting the discovery experience receives the largest share.
  • 15% — Artist Pool
    This portion is shared equally among eligible active artists participating in the applicable rotation.
  • 10% — Local Shuffle
    This supports the operation and development of the platform.

This describes the planned revenue model. Actual payment availability and distribution depend on implementation and the program's operating terms.

Why Local Businesses Matter

Independent businesses are gathering places for their communities. They give people a place to meet, relax, discover new things, and support the people around them.

Local Shuffle gives participating businesses another way to stand out while introducing customers to local artists. It is a small connection between two groups that deserve more attention: the people making music and the people building community.

We're starting in the Tampa Bay area and want to grow thoughtfully, one community and one participating location at a time.

Starting Local. Growing Together.

Local Shuffle starts with local artists and businesses. As the network grows, the goal is to make it easier for listeners to discover independent music wherever they go, while keeping the experience rooted in the communities that make it possible.

You don't have to know the band. You don't have to follow a trend. You just have to be willing to give a song a chance.
