Local Music ... Local Listeners

Become a Local Shuffle Artist

Local Shuffle helps local artists get their music into the hands of people who live, work, and hang out in their community.

Customers can add $1 to a qualifying purchase at a participating business and receive a randomly selected song from a Local Shuffle artist.

What Music Can Be Submitted?

Local Shuffle songs must be officially released and distributed music. The artist must have the legal right to provide the song to Local Shuffle for distribution to customers.

You'll be able to provide the actual MP3 file after your artist application has been approved.
