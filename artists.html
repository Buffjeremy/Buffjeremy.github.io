How Local Shuffle Supports Artists

Local Shuffle randomly connects listeners with music from participating local artists.

For the business-based Local Shuffle program, a portion of each participating $1 transaction is placed into an artist pool and divided among participating artists according to the Local Shuffle program rules.

Because the music is selected randomly, every participating artist has the opportunity to be discovered.
